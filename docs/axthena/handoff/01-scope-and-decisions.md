# Axthena — scope and current decision map

Consolidated decision map, 15 October 2026. Based on the full AX context through AX-054 and engineering context through ENG-043, plus later branch decisions through ENG-048 in `../repository-handoff.md`. The full six-field decisions are retained in `sources/Axthena-context.md` and `sources/Axthena-engineering-context.md`. Every AX-001–AX-054 and ENG-001–ENG-043 entry was reviewed. AX-016 is withdrawn; historically accepted entries are interpreted through their explicit later refinements. P-/F- sections are evidence of proposals/findings, not independent owner decisions.

## 1. Accepted current requirements, grouped by domain

### Ownership, scope and working method

- **Project and authority:** name is Axthena; keep its decisions separate from Qaedalus and Lexongine. Badr makes final architecture decisions. Assistant provides peer advice, concrete counterexamples and recorded reasoning. A reviewed written specification and execution plan precede production implementation. Record each accepted decision as Decision / Reason / Invariant / Trade-offs / Implementation scope / Required tests. **Sources: AX-001; ENG-001; AX “Project identity and boundaries”, “Discovery and review workflow”; ENG “Decision format”.**
- **Grounding and sequence:** develop the model from concrete evidence and useful reads; gather information before selecting mathematical representations. The initial useful query is selected below, but numerical encoding and consumer integration are not. **Sources: AX-002, AX-012, AX-049.**
- **V1 purpose:** collect, model, retain, compress and efficiently query stock information with understandable human/program outputs. Persistent evidence, explicit identities, sources, versions, relationships, configurable behavior and reproducible historical outputs are required. No data-science analysis, predictive-model training or empirical person scoring in V1; LLM-assisted extraction remains in scope. **Sources: AX-041; AX “Required properties supplied by the user”; ENG “Accepted V1 boundaries carried into design”.**
- **Initial universe:** Nvidia and Microsoft are the two primary stock targets; include relevant corporate organisations, suppliers, broad dependencies and competitors. Coverage is bounded, not an exhaustive global supply-chain crawl. Both targets need supported relationships valid for the selected historical interval. **Sources: AX-050, AX-051.**
- **Review style:** concise plain language, concrete examples and short numbered question batches; recommendations remain distinct from accepted answers. Earlier one-question-at-a-time wording is superseded. Autonomous research/design iterations were authorised; their recommendations were not thereby accepted. **Sources: ENG-001, ENG-015, ENG-030; AX “Discovery and review workflow”.**
- **Later research:** preserve the possible V2 cost/quality research path, but no adaptive policy, novelty, superiority or predictive advantage is established or selected. **Sources: AX-053; ENG-023.**

### Acquisition, local workflow and operational metadata

- **Local execution:** V1 runs on Badr's own computer with local persistent storage; no cross-machine serving requirement. This does not imply offline-only operation or local-only LLMs. Installation, restart persistence and eventual recovery must be tested on the chosen machine. **Source: ENG-012.**
- **Configuration and acquisition:** the analyst prepares the initial supplier/resource/topic coverage, possibly with research or AI help. V1 Axthena downloads configured source material itself, beginning with a news API; acquisition retains evidence before separately invocable processing. Automated discovery/maintenance of the surrounding-world configuration is not a V1 requirement. **Sources: ENG-009, ENG-010.**
- **Historical target and budget:** target twelve consecutive calendar months, with flexible exact bounds selected using coverage and cost, and the same interval framing both stocks with unequal coverage visible. Prefer free/minimal acquisition cost, under USD 20 if possible; USD 50 acceptable and potentially USD 100 if justified. This is neither purchase authority nor a recurring subscription commitment. Whether LLM/compute/storage are included is unresolved. **Sources: ENG-008, ENG-042.**
- **Prepare then read:** acquisition, processing and ordinary stored-data reads are separate; a read must not trigger external acquisition or missing-data extraction. V1 does not require reads during collection/processing. Future consumers may separately decide to request collection, but triggers and integrations are deferred. **Sources: ENG-004, ENG-013, ENG-014.**
- **Operational separation:** record collection scope/outcome so empty reads can distinguish missing coverage, failure and collected-but-no-match. Keep acquisition/processing/query operational metadata logically separate from news/events/facts/opinions; failure must not become an invented domain record. Exact scope/status/output rules remain to be selected. **Sources: ENG-005, ENG-006.**
- **Failure and adequacy:** retain acquired originals even when extraction fails; retries reuse evidence and do not create another originating publication. Encode general insufficient-information rules with understandable reasons, distinct from operational failure, no-match and conflicting confirmation. Optional missing stock direction does not invalidate a report. Existing labelled dummy/default allowances remain valid. **Sources: ENG-007, ENG-010, ENG-011.**
- **Partial extraction:** a valid extracted Idea is readable even if another selected Idea fails. Incompleteness remains visible in separate operational information. All-failed work must not fabricate a completed result; retries must not duplicate successful results or publication counts. **Source: ENG-036; refinement of ENG-007/ENG-011.**
- **Edits:** active rechecking, revision discovery and detailed article-edit handling are V2. Retain the exact source content actually used and its passage/provenance links in V1; no complete edit history or unavailable original version may be claimed. The separate-linked-revision concept is preserved for V2. **Sources: ENG-002, ENG-003; AX-052.**

### Stocks, organisations, actors, domains and dependencies

- **Distinct identities:** a stock is distinct from an organisation and can point to several organisations. Corporate-group association remains distinct from supplier dependency. Supply alone does not prove membership; separate supported membership and supply may coexist. Stock/issuer/subsidiary lookup details remain open. **Sources: AX-042, AX-043.**
- **Broad value creation:** organisations have distinct domains/activities, each with broad processes, inputs, resources, outputs and dependencies; preserve what a dependency means and its evidence. Domains need not be independent or connected without support. **Sources: AX-011, AX-013, AX-014.**
- **Reusable activity categories:** organisations may share an activity category such as AI chips while retaining organisation-specific activities, suppliers and exposure. Category sharing must not transfer dependencies or corporate membership between organisations. **Source: ENG-032.**
- **Subjects and relationships:** one opinion/Idea may concern several organisations with the proper organisation–domain pairings. A joint claim stays one opinion/Idea unless the article develops distinct meanings. Events concern organisations/domains; person attribution complements report attachment. **Sources: AX-019, AX-020, AX-021; ENG-031, ENG-032, ENG-035.**
- **Indirect relevance:** a report/opinion may link to an indirectly relevant organisation through a dated supported dependency path. Label it Axthena interpretation and retain the full supporting path. Relevance is not realised impact, causation or a projected future world. Depth/cycle/query controls await technical design. **Sources: AX-022, AX-045.**
- **Anonymous people:** a mass person is an anonymous individual, not one aggregate crowd. Preserve stable available account references; do not invent real identities or merge separate anonymous accounts without evidence. Cross-source account linking and completely unattributed speakers remain open. **Source: AX-023.**
- **Role and weight scope:** game-role classifications are excluded from V1. Use one global, domain-independent person vector with separate credibility, influence and predictive-record components. All may use available clearly labelled dummy/provisional values with origin/version/time; absence is not permission to fabricate inputs. Weights prove neither truth, influence, intent nor predictive usefulness. **Sources: AX-019, AX-020, AX-040, AX-041.**

### Organisation identity and Idea preparation

- **Case consistency:** resolve organisation case variants to the same identity before organisation-based candidate filtering and Idea classification. Preserve original spelling and distinct parent/subsidiary/stock identities. Unrelated same-name organisations are a V2 disambiguation topic; names are not thereby guaranteed globally unique. **Source: ENG-019.**
- **Retrieval and decision separation:** perform name-similarity and semantic/vector retrieval separately over existing organisation IDs. Give their identity-deduplicated union to AI for the V1 match decision. Preserve each method's candidates, overlap/intersection membership and matching/provenance context for later comparison. Retrieval overlap does not establish identity or accuracy. **Sources: ENG-020, ENG-024.**
- **Automatic identity continuation:** reuse a selected existing identity; otherwise automatically create a separate organisation, including completed uncertain comparisons, without mandatory personal review. Preserve uncertainty and alternatives. Retrieval/extraction/tool failure is not a completed no-match decision. Creation does not verify a distinct legal entity or confirm claims. Repeated processing must not duplicate the same successful creation. **Sources: ENG-025, ENG-026.**
- **Creation-time shared meaning:** retain every original and source Expression; during processing seek existing equivalent shared Ideas instead of automatically duplicating meaning. Grouping belongs to preparation. Preserve independent reports/people/expressions and historical grouping changes. Shared topic, organisation, direction or timing alone is insufficient for equivalence. **Sources: AX-024; ENG-018.**
- **First Idea filter:** retain candidates sharing at least one concerned stable organisation identity with the incoming Idea; deduplicate candidates. Subsequent comparison checks the complete claim, organisation roles, direction and forecast window. Missing organisation extraction is not established empty overlap. **Source: ENG-031.**
- **No flattening of different meanings:** opposing judgments, different actual forecast windows, different subjects/scopes and materially different complete opinions remain distinct. V1's deferral of a dedicated reason subsystem does not permit merging different complete meanings solely because stock/direction/period agree. **Sources: AX-024, AX-027, AX-037, AX-054; ENG-018, ENG-022, ENG-028.**

### Reports, news, facts, confirmation and events

- **Distinct concepts:** original report, source statement, Event, Fact/Confirmation, Idea/Expression and derived interpretation must remain distinguishable. A report is not the fact it reports. Confirmed information belongs under facts with precise claim, evidence, external confirming system/process and relevant time. A known statement being made is distinct from confirmation of its contents. **Sources: AX-003, AX-018, AX-033; ENG “Accepted V1 boundaries carried into design”.**
- **News and opinion:** organisational news can be rumoured or confirmed; personal opinions remain distinct and outside that news-status split. Retain who speaks. Source category by itself is not confirmation. **Sources: AX-005 as refined by AX-033.**
- **Origins and scoped confirmation:** company/authorised representative information can originate company facts; recognised institutions can confirm relevant external occurrences. An institution confirming an earthquake does not also confirm transport disruption, supplier damage, company impact or price movement. The detailed qualifying authority/process list remains unselected. **Sources: AX-003, AX-033, AX-035.**
- **Confidential-source reports:** “people familiar with the talks” remains rumoured until qualifying confirmation. Retain publisher and source description without fabricating the unnamed identity. Rumoured does not mean false. **Source: AX-034.**
- **Conflict:** genuine external confirmation disagreement on the same claim requires on-hold status and dated personal review. No automatic fact-conflict resolution. Preserve conflicting evidence and earlier historical state after later review. Exact hold read policy and review outcomes remain open. **Source: AX-004.**
- **Event identity:** an announcement and a later production start are distinct events with their own reports, evidence and times; shared subject does not merge them. Event-occurrence confirmation is separate from possible company effect and realised effect. Direct event-to-event linkage is not selected. **Sources: AX-035, AX-044.**
- **No projected world:** gathered future-oriented claims remain dated reports/judgments. Do not create expected-future organisation states, event outcomes or supplier dependencies. External-event forecast modelling is deferred; retained earthquake-prediction wording is not confirmed occurrence. **Sources: AX-016 withdrawn; AX-018, AX-036.**

### Supported extraction, judgments, agreement and spread

- **Idea extraction:** use LLMs to select the most articulated ideas rather than blindly follow headlines. Current V1 cap is at most two separately developed Ideas; do not fabricate a second or split merely because two stocks are mentioned. Preserve the complete original, exact supporting passages, separate summaries/subjects and applicable judgments. Cap omissions are not extraction failures. **Sources: AX-008, AX-009 as refined by AX-038; ENG-035.**
- **Evidence presentation:** each short summary must resolve to exact supporting passages in the correct retained version and preserve material qualifications. Selection and valid quotation do not establish truth. **Sources: AX-009, AX-010.**
- **Optional stock judgments:** news/opinions may have judgments, but V1's directional output is supported expected stock-price UP/DOWN only. Attach it to the report/Idea, target stock and responsible available source/person. No clear direction means no directional judgment, not flat price, zero agreement or useless data. Business growth alone never implies stock UP. **Sources: AX-006, AX-007, AX-029, AX-030.**
- **Quoted-judgment exclusion:** omit other people's judgments quoted within a report from V1 judgment extraction and expressed-agreement scoring; retain original quoted material. Do not transfer the quote to the publisher, encode a zero/absence-of-agreement measurement, or replace a more articulated quotation-dominated idea just to obtain a score. This is not a ban on first-party statements, whole articles or original evidence. **Source: AX-026.**
- **Period provenance:** retain explicit periods; otherwise attempt wording-based inference; otherwise use configurable near-term fallback. Record source-stated, inferred or configured origin and the original wording/configuration. Explicitly mark unstated forecast information, distinguishing no supported forecast from a supported direction with unstated period. Seven days is tentative, not accepted. **Sources: AX-031; ENG-034.**
- **Forecast windows:** relative wording anchors to source publication when no different explicit reference is supplied. One unqualified week means seven calendar days; two means fourteen, with trading-bar mapping separate. Different actual windows stay separate even with identical relative text or overlap. Equivalent same-fixed-window forecasts may share meaning across publication dates. **Sources: AX-027; ENG-028, ENG-029, ENG-033.**
- **Expressed agreement:** weak/moderate/strong measures the source's expressed agreement with the particular Idea on its Expression link. It is distinct from person weight, evidence quantity, truth, sincerity, causality and AI confidence. Strong agreement with DOWN is strong agreement, not positive direction. Missing/excluded/unclear agreement is not automatically weak or zero. **Sources: AX-025, AX-039.**
- **Spread:** retain distinct collected publication mentions and supported origins; copies are not independent confirmation, and shared meaning does not prove copying. Reprocessing one report does not create another publication. Counts describe observed coverage in selected sources, not audience size or global exposure. **Sources: AX-024, AX-032.**
- **Reason scope:** preserve source wording, including expressed reasons, and distinguish materially different complete opinions. Dedicated reason/thought-process extraction, matching and shared reason records are V2. AI processing rationale/provenance remains required in V1 and is not the deferred reason subsystem. **Sources: AX-037, AX-054; ENG-021 as refined by ENG-022.**
- **AI lineage:** retain evidence/version/passages, supplied inputs/context, instructions/version, model/version when available, output, actual processing time and short wording-grounded explanation. Preserve stored historical outputs rather than silently rerun today's model. Explanations are not model internals or private motives. **Source: AX-028.**

### Time, views and reads

- **Source-first retrospective history:** seek first source availability for V1 source-aligned reads. Supported Monday original collected Wednesday remains Monday source information. Do not substitute Wednesday-corrected wording under a Monday page date. Actual collection and processing remain separate; uncertain availability remains uncertain. This does not establish globally earliest reporting or operational possession/computation at the historical date. **Sources: AX-047, AX-052, superseding AX-046's collection-only inclusion for this use.**
- **Historical integrity:** source date, first-supported availability, event/reporting-period date, collection and processing are distinguishable. Later confirmations, reviews, relationships, grouping decisions, fitted classifications, view interpretations and configuration changes must not silently enter earlier outputs. Every supporting dependency/claim link needs relevant temporal eligibility, not merely an old root article. Exact derived-output/backtesting contracts are still specification work. **Sources: AX-012, AX-015, AX-017, AX-018, AX-020, AX-022, AX-024, AX-028, AX-031, AX-047, AX-048, AX-052; ENG-018, ENG-028, ENG-038.**
- **OHLCV alignment:** information opening/closing pictures map to selected OHLCV intervals. Five trading days was an example, not fixed calendar blocks; daily OHLCV is the main focus. Preserve source/event/availability timestamps and do not move later information to an earlier boundary. Exact market calendar/timezones/cutoffs/bar conventions remain open. Detailed operational derived-readiness latency is V2; zero latency is not promised. **Sources: AX-015, AX-017, AX-047.**
- **Views:** operate over shared evidence with explicit selected sources, interpretations and versions. A country label does not mean all national media, agreement among outlets or representative/neutral feeds. Read mechanics remain technical design work. **Source: AX-048.**
- **Core reads:** customisable stock/date-range reads return relevant events, facts, news and opinions with evidence and organisation/domain links; preserve judgments/status/attribution/versions/views/dependency paths. Support prepared direct opinion lookups over configured market/universe instead of repeatedly finding everything through subsidiaries. Query language and physical indexes are not selected. **Sources: AX-049; ENG-018.**
- **Manual two-stock view:** investigator selects two stocks and reads evidence concerning either plus supported shared events, competition/dependencies; retain joint source statements and per-stock supported judgment/windows. “A outperforms B” does not imply A UP/B DOWN. No automatic pair discovery, Pair node, strategy computation or convergence claim is selected. **Source: ENG-027.**

### Implementation language, LLM boundary and experimental evidence

- **C++ main application:** collection, processing, validation, storage integration and query application are substantively C++; not a token C++ wrapper over Python. Standard, build system, libraries and backend are still unselected. Eventual checks include native build, owned multirow results/resource lifetime, provenance replay, read-only enforcement, recovery and stage-separated local performance. **Source: ENG-016.**
- **Replaceable LLM communication boundary:** separate communication/provider capabilities from information semantics and acceptance/validation. Preserve exact request/response and model/configuration/attempt lineage. Communication success is distinct from accepted extraction; saved response replay is distinct from fresh generation. Concrete types/APIs/retry/budget/providers remain proposals. **Source: ENG-038.**
- **Authorised research and experiments:** research extraction methods/model benchmarks before instructions; current ChatGPT subscription was selected for the first experiment. Bounded A/B/C synthetic extraction/comparison trials and harder samples were authorised, preserving frozen references/prompts/raw outputs and honest provenance limits. This does not choose a production model, prompt/schema, provider API, runtime or performance threshold. **Sources: ENG-037, ENG-039, ENG-040, ENG-041.**
- **Graph-first validation:** populated Cypher review and then graph output produced from draft extraction are authorised review milestones. Badr inspects meanings/relationships/queries before downstream representation, abstraction and optimisation choices. Fictional evidence remains fictional; a Cypher artifact does not choose a database. **Sources: ENG-017, ENG-043.**

## 3. Proposals, open selections, defects and deferred work

One authoritative store plus archive, immutable generations, typed bounded queries and Zstandard are advisor proposals. **Sources: ENG-012/016; P-006/P-008; AX-041/043/045; ENG “Technical-design work to resolve”.**

7. **Read/output contract:** supported filters/shapes, source/view scope, two-stock semantics, historical inclusion of every supporting link, on-hold read behavior, missing/partial coverage output, one answer per identity despite multiple proof paths, cycle/depth/resource bounds, unsupported cutoff response, evidence inspection and direct market-opinion lookup.
